# Moodforest Design System
Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

Moodforest exists to make complex biological recovery understandable, trustworthy and beautiful.

Every page should feel calm, intelligent and evidence-led.

The design should never feel clinical, noisy or sales driven.

---

# Design Principles

## 1. Evidence before claims

Always show measured observations before explaining them.

Good

Observed participant outcomes

Bad

The world's best fasting programme.

---

## 2. Simplicity creates trust

Every section should communicate one idea.

If a section explains two ideas, split it.

---

## 3. Whitespace communicates confidence

Never fill empty space.

Generous spacing is preferred over more information.

---

## 4. Numbers tell stories

Never display numbers without context.

Instead of

22%

Write

Average LDL Reduction

22%

---

## 5. Human first

Visitors should understand the page before they understand the science.

---

## 6. Scientific honesty

Avoid exaggerated language.

Preferred

Observed

Measured

Average

Participants

Appears

May

Avoid

Proven

Guaranteed

Miracle

Revolutionary

---

# Living Evidence Philosophy

The page should answer these questions in order.

1. Is this real?

2. What changed?

3. Why does it matter?

4. How was it measured?

5. Could this work for me?

Never change this order.
