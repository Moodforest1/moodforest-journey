Is SuperFasting Right for Me?

Understanding comes before participation.

SuperFasting is not designed for everyone. Our first priority is helping people understand whether the programme is appropriate for them.

Who may benefit

  • People who no longer feel completely like themselves.
  • Those seeking renewed vitality.
  • People curious about understanding their biology.

Additional guidance may be needed

  • Complex medical conditions.
  • Prescription medications requiring supervision.
  • Pregnancy or special nutritional needs.

Our philosophy

Suitability is more important than enrolment. Understanding always comes before participation.
